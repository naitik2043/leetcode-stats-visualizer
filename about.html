ABOUT LEETMETRIC

A better way to
read your progress.

LeetMetric is an independent project built around one simple idea: coding progress becomes more useful when your data is understandable, actionable, and connected to the habits behind it.

ClarityTurn raw profile numbers into a readable snapshot.
ConsistencyFocus on sustainable practice, not vanity metrics.
OwnershipYour account and saved preferences stay under your control.
OUR APPROACH

Numbers are a starting point, not the whole story.

LeetMetric begins with public LeetCode statistics: solved problems, difficulty distribution, and submission activity. The product roadmap expands this into historical progress, profile comparison, and personalized learning guidance.

We aim to keep official platform metrics distinct from generated recommendations, and to be transparent about how account data is handled.

WHAT WE VALUE

Useful by default. Honest by design.

We avoid fake rankings, fabricated metrics, and promises that features are live before they are ready. LeetMetric is not affiliated with LeetCode.
