LEGAL / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: November 28, 2026. This policy describes the information handled by the current LeetMetric product and the account features being introduced.

1. Information we handle

When you look up a public LeetCode username, the app sends that username to its server-side lookup endpoint, which requests public profile statistics from LeetCode. A lookup does not require your LeetCode password.

If account services are enabled, we may store your name, email address, password hash, saved LeetCode username, account timestamps, and password-reset token metadata. Passwords must be stored as salted hashes and are never stored in plaintext.

When you explicitly save a progress snapshot while signed in, we store the public profile metrics returned for your saved LeetCode username, including total solved counts, difficulty counts, available question totals, submission totals, acceptance rate, and the UTC snapshot date. We also store goals you create, including their metric, target, timeframe, and baseline value, plus revision-queue entries and any notes you choose to write. Revision entries may include a problem title, optional public problem URL, topic, difficulty, mistake category and your personal notes. We do not collect your LeetCode password, session cookie, private submissions or source code.

Messages submitted through the contact form may include your name, email address, subject, message, and creation timestamp.

If you open Code Vault from your signed-in dashboard, we store the code and text snippets you choose to save, including titles, programming language, LeetCode problem context, topic labels, solution approach/intuition, time and space complexity notes, key takeaways, tags, visibility setting, pin state and prior versions. New snippets are private to your account. When you explicitly create an unlisted share link, anyone with that link can view the shared snippet. Do not store passwords, API keys, private keys, session tokens or sensitive personal data in Code Vault.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide account access, save a preferred public profile, respond to contact requests, secure the service, and maintain the product. Public profile data is used to render the requested statistics.

3. Cookies and authentication

Authenticated sessions use an HttpOnly, Secure-in-production, SameSite cookie. The cookie is not exposed to browser JavaScript. You can sign out to clear the session cookie.

4. Service providers

LeetMetric and the separate Paste Vault web app are hosted through Vercel. Account, analytics, revision and snippet records are stored in the configured MongoDB database. The two apps use a short-lived, one-time launch ticket to hand off an authenticated LeetMetric identity to Paste Vault; Paste Vault then issues its own HttpOnly session cookie. Public profile requests are sent to LeetCode. Password reset delivery, when enabled, is handled by the configured email provider.

5. Retention and deletion

Progress snapshots are automatically scheduled for deletion 180 days after creation. The dashboard exposes the most recent 180 saved daily snapshots. Practice goals remain in your account until you delete them or request account-data deletion. Account and contact records are retained while needed to provide the service. Signed-in users can request permanent account deletion from Account Settings after confirming their account email and current password. The deletion endpoint removes the user record and associated snapshots, goals, revision-queue items, Code Vault snippets and unused launch tickets in a MongoDB transaction. Backup and provider retention windows may affect how quickly deletion propagates.

6. Security

We use password hashing, server-side validation, restricted cookies, request limits, and secret management as appropriate. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Never send us your passwords or session cookies.

7. Children's privacy

LeetMetric is a general developer tool and is not designed to collect personal data from children. Do not create an account if you are not permitted to do so under applicable law.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be revised as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page. Use the contact page for privacy questions.

Note: This is a product privacy notice, not legal advice. The operator should review it for the jurisdictions and actual data practices that apply before opening the service broadly.
